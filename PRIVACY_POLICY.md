## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information

When you create a Zenify account, we collect:

- **Email address** — To authenticate and recover your account
- **Username** — For your profile
- **Password** — Encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt
- **Profile picture** — Optional, stored securely
- **Account creation date** — Automatically recorded

**We do NOT collect**:
- Real name (optional if you provide it)
- Phone number
- Location/IP address (unless you share it)
- Payment information (we're free, no payment processing)

---

### 2. Listening Data

Your music preferences are stored **locally on your device by default**.

#### What's Local (On Your Device)
- Listening history
- Favorite tracks and playlists
- Search history
- Play counts and statistics
- Ratings and reviews

These are **never sent to our servers** unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

#### What's Cloud-Synced (Only With Permission)
If you enable cloud sync, the following is encrypted and stored on our servers:
- Playlist names and descriptions
- Following/followers list
- Public playlists you share
- Profile customizations

You can **disable cloud sync anytime** and all synced data is deleted from our servers.

---

### 3. Usage Analytics (Optional)

We collect **anonymized, aggregated** usage data only if you opt-in:

- App crash reports (no personal data included)
- Feature usage patterns
- Performance metrics
- Device type and OS version

**We do NOT collect**:
- Individual track listening history
- Your identity linked to usage
- IP addresses
- Geolocation data

---

### 4. Communication Data

When you contact us, we collect:
- Your email address
- Message content
- Support tickets and conversations
- Feedback and feature requests

This data is used only to respond to your inquiries and improve our service.

## Data Retention

### Account Data
- Stored as long as your account exists
- Automatically deleted 30 days after account deletion
- Can be manually requested for deletion anytime

### Local Data (On Your Device)
- Stored indefinitely on your device
- Deleted only when you manually clear cache or uninstall
- **We have no access to this data**

### Cloud-Synced Data
- Stored encrypted on our servers
- Can be deleted anytime in settings
- Automatically deleted 90 days after account deletion

### Support Communications
- Retained for 2 years for support purposes
- Can be deleted anytime by requesting data deletion

### Analytics Data
- Aggregated and anonymized automatically
- Deleted after 90 days
- Never linked to your identity

## Security

### How We Protect Your Data

1. **End-to-End Encryption**
   - Cloud-synced data encrypted before leaving your device
   - Encryption keys never shared with us
   - Only your device can decrypt your data

2. **Secure Authentication**
   - Passwords hashed with bcrypt (not reversible)
   - Optional two-factor authentication (2FA)
   - Session tokens expire after 24 hours

3. **Server Security**
   - All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
   - Servers protected by firewalls and DDoS mitigation
   - Regular security audits by third-party experts
   - Bug bounty program for responsible disclosure

4. **Code Review**
   - Open-source code auditable by anyone
   - Security updates released within 48 hours of discovery
   - All contributors verified

### What You Can Do

- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Keep your device updated
- Don't share your login credentials
- Report security issues responsibly

## Your Rights & Choices

### Right to Access
You can request a copy of all your data:

1. Go to **Settings → Privacy**
2. Click **"Download My Data"**
3. Receive a complete export in JSON format within 7 days

---

### Right to Delete
You can delete your account and all associated data:

1. Go to **Settings → Account**
2. Click **"Delete Account"**
3. Confirm deletion (irreversible)
4. All data deleted from our servers within 30 days

---

### Right to Opt-Out
You can opt-out of optional data collection:

- **Cloud Sync**: Settings → Privacy → Disable Cloud Sync
- **Analytics**: Settings → Privacy → Disable Usage Analytics
- **Notifications**: Settings → Notifications → Customize

---

### Right to Correct
You can update your information anytime:

- Email: Settings → Account → Change Email
- Username: Settings → Profile → Edit Username
- Profile Picture: Settings → Profile → Upload Photo
- Password: Settings → Security → Change Password

---

### Right to Data Portability
Your data belongs to you. You can export it in multiple formats:

- **JSON**: Full export of all data
- **CSV**: Listening history and playlists
- **OPML**: Podcast subscriptions (if applicable)

## European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you're in the EU/UK, GDPR applies to your data:

### Legal Basis for Processing
- **Consent**: Cloud sync, analytics (you opt-in)
- **Contractual**: Account data needed to provide service
- **Legal Obligation**: Comply with law enforcement
- **Legitimate Interest**: Security, fraud prevention

### Data Protection Rights
- Right to access your data
- Right to rectify inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to lodge a complaint with a DPA (Data Protection Authority)

### Data Transfer
Zenify processes data in compliance with GDPR. Any international data transfers include appropriate safeguards.

**Your DPA**:
- EU: Your country's data protection authority
- UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
