# 📚 Zenify Usage Examples

Real-world scenarios and how to use Zenify for different purposes.

---

## 🎧 Scenario 1: Casual Listening

**Who**: Someone who just wants to play music without ads or paywalls

**Steps**:
1. Open https://listenzenify.vercel.app
2. Search "Blinding Lights"
3. Click play
4. Enjoy ad-free music

**Why Zenify**: Free, no login, no ads interrupting

---

## 🎛️ Scenario 2: Audio Enthusiast (Custom EQ)

**Who**: Someone who wants professional audio quality

**Steps**:
1. Play a song
2. Click settings icon (gear)
3. Click "Audio Effects"
4. Adjust EQ: Boost bass (+3dB), cut mid (-2dB), boost treble (+2dB)
5. Apply preset: "Bass Boost"
6. Notice the difference immediately

**Why Zenify**: Professional EQ not available on Spotify (premium-only)

---

## 🎤 Scenario 3: Karaoke Night

**Who**: Planning a karaoke session with friends

**Steps**:
1. Play "Don't Stop Believin'"
2. Click lyrics icon
3. Enable "Karaoke Mode" (word-level highlighting)
4. Use "Vocal Isolation" in audio effects
5. Use "Speed Control" to slow down (0.8x speed)
6. Project on TV or screen
7. Start singing!

**Why Zenify**: Only music player with vocal isolation + synced lyrics + speed control together

---

## 🎵 Scenario 4: Local Music Library

**Who**: Has MP3s and wants to play them in Zenify

**Steps**:
1. Prepare MP3 files with ID3 tags (artist, title, album art)
2. Open Zenify
3. Click "Upload Local Files" (or drag-drop)
4. Select your MP3s
5. Zenify extracts metadata automatically
6. Play with full audio effects

**Why Zenify**: Only free player that lets you use all effects on local files

---

## 🌙 Scenario 5: Late Night Listening

**Who**: Wants comfortable audio for late-night sessions

**Steps**:
1. Play your favorite songs
2. Enable "Night Mode" in audio effects
3. Adjust compression settings
4. Lower volume
5. Zenify applies dynamic compression for comfortable listening

**Why Zenify**: Protects ears with dynamic compression (Spotify doesn't have this)

## 🎸 Scenario 8: DJ Night (Speed/Pitch Control)

**Who**: Wants to remix songs during a DJ set

**Steps**:
1. Play a song
2. Use "Speed Control": slow to 0.8x for buildup
3. Use "Pitch Shift": up +2 semitones for energy
4. Layer with effects: reverb + echo
5. Live mix on the fly

**Why Zenify**: DJ controls in a free web player (usually desktop-only software)

---

## 🌐 Scenario 10: Offline Travel

**Who**: Traveling with limited internet

**Before Trip**:
1. Open Zenify (online)
2. Play 10-20 songs you like
3. They cache locally
4. Install Zenify as PWA

**During Trip**:
1. Open Zenify (offline mode)
2. All cached songs still play
3. All effects still work
4. No internet needed

**Why Zenify**: Only music player that works completely offline

---

## 💾 Scenario 11: Self-Hosted Private Music

**Who**: Wants complete privacy and control

**Setup**:
```bash
docker-compose up
# Zenify runs on your local server
```

**Benefits**:
- Your data, your server
- No tracking
- No ads
- No third-party access
- Share with family/friends on your network

**Why Zenify**: Only free, open-source, self-hostable music player

## 🎁 Bonus: Tips & Tricks

### Tip 1: Audio Preset Creation
Save your favorite EQ settings as custom presets

### Tip 2: Keyboard Shortcuts
- Space: Play/Pause
- Right arrow: Next song
- Left arrow: Previous song
- M: Mute

### Tip 3: Offline First
Add songs to offline cache before traveling

### Tip 4: EQ Matching
Match EQ settings to your headphone type (gaming, studio, beats)

### Tip 5: 8D Audio
Works best with headphones (spatial effects)
