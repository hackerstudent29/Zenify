## ✨ What You Get with Zenify

* **Full Songs Only (Zero Snippets)** — Streams full-length high-bitrate tracks (up to 320 kbps) with smooth loudness normalization and stereo/mono support.
* **Synced & Static Lyrics** — Word-by-word glowing karaoke lyrics, multi-language romanization, manual timing adjustment, and a clean static reading view.
* **20 DJ Crossfades & Studio FX** — Blend tracks smoothly with 20 transition styles, parametric EQ, spatial audio, reverb, and live timeline previews.
* **Music Videos & Offline Listening** — Stream 16:9 music videos or download songs, artwork, lyrics, and videos for zero-data offline playback.
* **Room Together & Story Sharing** — Listen in sync with friends via voice chat and emoji reactions, or export custom story cards for Instagram and WhatsApp.
* **Privacy & System Polished** — Supports lock-screen media controls, Bluetooth head units, headphone pause/resume, audio-focus auto-resume, sleep timer, and zero telemetry.
