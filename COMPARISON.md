## 💰 Price & Value

### Spotify
- **Cost**: $12/month (or $120/year)
- **Value**: Massive music library, good recommendations
- **Downside**: Expensive, tracks you, has ads in free tier

### Apple Music
- **Cost**: $10/month
- **Value**: Integrates with Apple ecosystem
- **Downside**: Expensive, limited compatibility, tracks you

### YouTube Music
- **Cost**: $12/month (or free with ads)
- **Value**: YouTube integration, free tier available
- **Downside**: Ad-supported, bloated with videos, tracks you

### JioSaavn
- **Cost**: Free (or ₹99/month premium)
- **Value**: Regional focus, Indian music focus
- **Downside**: Limited international, tracks you (Jio ecosystem)

### **Zenify**
- **Cost**: **FREE forever**
- **Value**: Professional audio, regional languages, offline, privacy
- **Upside**: No ads, no tracking, open source, works everywhere

**Annual savings vs Spotify**: **$144**

### For Privacy Advocates
```
Spotify tracks every song you play and sells the data.
Zenify tracks nothing. Open source. Completely private.
```

### For Audio Enthusiasts
```
Spotify has basic controls. Zenify has professional audio tools:
- 3-band EQ, 8D audio, vocal isolation, effects, presets
All completely free.
```

### For Budget-Conscious Users
```
Spotify costs $144/year. Zenify costs $0.
That's your money saved. Forever.
```

### For Regional Language Speakers
```
Spotify doesn't support Tamil/Hindi/Malayalam lyrics.
Zenify specializes in regional languages + 50+ global languages.
```

### For Offline Users
```
Spotify offline requires premium ($15+/mo).
Zenify works offline completely free.
```

### For Developers & Contributors
```
Spotify is closed-source, proprietary.
Zenify is 100% open source. Build on top, contribute, improve.
```

Can't self-host Spotify or Apple Music.
