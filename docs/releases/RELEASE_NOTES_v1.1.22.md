# Zenify v1.1.22

## What's New
- **Smarter Single, Album & Video Song Browsing**:
  - Opening a song from Singles now opens its Single view with a quick option to jump to the artist or full album instead of misrouting to an unrelated release.
  - Dedicated Video Single view with recommended video songs, artist links, cleaner titles and artist names, release year details, and better organization across Home.
- **Full Song Credits & Static Lyrics View**:
  - Added a dedicated Song Credits page showing performing artists, cast, composers, lyricists, production credits, release details, and quick access to full lyrics.
  - Added a clean, static full-screen Lyrics view with clear formatting and zero distractions.
- **Smooth Portrait ↔ Landscape Player & Video Controls**:
  - Crisp 90° rotation transition when switching between portrait and landscape player modes.
  - Landscape video playback now includes ±10s skip controls, double-tap seeking, and a fit/fill zoom toggle.
- **Transparent Liquid Glass Look**:
  - Refined Top Picks and hero banners with a cleaner transparent liquid-glass style.
- **New Zendrum App Icon & Visual Identity**:
  - Updated the app icon and brand marks with the new rose Zendrum emblem.

## Fixes & Improvements
- **Audio & Video Playback Reliability**:
  - Fixed playback initialization issues for smoother, faster audio and video startup.
- **Persistent Dock & MiniPlayer Navigation**:
  - Moving between Credits, Lyrics, Artists, and Albums now keeps the bottom Dock and MiniPlayer visible and accessible.
- **Better Bottom Spacing**:
  - Improved bottom scroll padding across detail pages so content never hides behind the Dock or MiniPlayer.
- **General Performance & Cleanup**:
  - Streamlined background services for faster startup and cleaner app behavior.
